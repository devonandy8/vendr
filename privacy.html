Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026. This policy describes how Vendr handles information in the campus marketplace app and on this website.

Who we are

Vendr is a campus marketplace for university students. Contact us at support@campusmarket.app.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Vendr, we may collect account details (name, email, campus), listings you post, messages, order and payout records, delivery locations you choose, and device information needed to run the app.

How we use it

We use this information to operate the marketplace, process payments with Paystack, show relevant campus listings, provide support, and keep the community safe.

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure and payment processors that help us run Vendr. We do not sell personal information. Other students may see the profile and listing details you choose to publish.

This website

The public website stores waitlist emails, demo carts, and support drafts in your browser only (local storage). Those demo records are not sent to our servers unless you email support.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by emailing support@campusmarket.app. You can also clear this website's local demo data from your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@campusmarket.app.

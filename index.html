How Vendr works

One campus profile. Buy, sell, book, eat, and stay without switching identities.

1

Join with your campus.

Sign in, pick your university, and land in the market that actually belongs to your school.

2

Find it or list it.

Browse products, services, food, and rooms — or post yours in a flow built for that experience.

3

Pay, chat, deliver.

Message in context, pay with Paystack, and move things by pickup, rider, or campus drop.

Yes. This is the public homepage for Vendr, the campus marketplace app formerly prototyped as Campus Market.

No. The market on this page is a local demo. Orders and support tickets stay in your browser so you can try the flow.

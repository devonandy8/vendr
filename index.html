How Vendr works

One campus profile. Buy, sell, book, eat, and stay without switching identities.

1

Join your campus

Sign in, select your university, and enter the marketplace scoped to your school.

2

Browse or list

Discover products, services, food, and rooms — or publish your own listing in a guided flow.

3

Pay, chat, deliver

Message in context, checkout with Paystack, and coordinate pickup or campus delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official Vendr website?

Yes. This is the public site for Vendr, the campus marketplace app for university students in Ghana.

Does the demo charge real money?

No. The marketplace demo on this page runs entirely in your browser. Orders are saved locally and no payment is processed.

Join the waitlist below. When your campus opens, we'll send iOS and Android download links to your email.

Visit the support page or email support@campusmarket.app.
